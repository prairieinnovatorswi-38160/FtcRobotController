BIOBUZZ Sim · rules & controls
You drive RED (red alliance on the left, audience wall at the bottom). Field per the 2026-27 manual: 144 in, 36 tiles, centre HIVE frame (49.46 × 38.95 in, pivot 43.95 in), a FLOWER on each wall, gardens, loading zones, staging per §10.3.1.

Robot: REV DUO FTC Starter Bot (2026-27). Channel Drivetrain (4 × 408 mm C-channel rails, 20:1 HD Hex gearing, 90 mm traction / grip / omni wheels), Core Hex intake with six flap wheels (shaft 88 mm off the floor, 54-link #25 chain), polycarbonate chute, 2 × 90 mm grip-wheel flywheel on an HD Hex motor, Smart Robot Servo feeder (crServo) and a Smart Robot Servo pollen poker (pollenServo) on the side. The intake faces the wall at the start and the launcher fires out of the back.

Hardware config (REV): leftDrive rightDrive (reversed) intakeMotor flywheel crServo pollenServo. The two presets are REV's own OnBot Java TeleOp and Auto, unchanged (targetVelocity = 1160).

Gamepad (REV layout): left stick arcade drive · RB intake · RT outtake · LB flywheel sequence (spin, then feed once within ±20 ticks) · Y flywheel only · X flywheel reverse (−0.55) · D-pad ↑/↓ feeder · A poker down (position 0) · B poker up (position 1).

Pollen poker (side-mounted servo, rod with an inward hook): A lowers it (pollenServo 0), B raises it (1). It is not a hammer: nothing happens unless the hook touches the pollen. Lower it, put the hook in a flower's bottom opening and turn or drive so it sweeps across; it pushes the bottom POLLEN sideways out of its ring, toward the intake (run RB to catch it). Let go and the pollen rolls back. NECTAR (3.6 in) cannot pass the opening (G418).

Rules enforced: G418 (enter flowers only through the top; remove only POLLEN from the bottom), G410 (NECTAR into a flower before the last 60 s = MAJOR FOUL, +20 blue), G407 (max 4 controlled), R105.

Score: HIVE TIP +20, element in your cell +2, garden +1, flowers: elements above the middle ring count for the owner (+2 each, +5 bottom-nectar bonus), leave 3, park 5.

Vision: real 36h11 AprilTags (3.25 in), IDs 30–33 / 34–37 on the red cells and 38–41 / 42–45 on the blue. The C920 is an optional add-on (📷 panel). Program it with VisionPortal + AprilTagProcessor (preset “REV + AprilTag aim”).

Keys: W/A/S/D left stick · E = RB intake · R = RT outtake · Q = LB flywheel sequence · T = Y · Z = X · ↑/↓ = D-pad feeder · F = A poker down · G = B poker up. Xbox / USB gamepads are read automatically and shown live on the controller. 🧊 toggles 2D, 🎯 cycles cameras, “Code” opens the editor on small screens.

Viewer: mouse wheel / pinch = zoom, drag = pan, right-drag or Shift-drag = orbit (✥ switches one-finger drag between pan and orbit), double-click or ⟲ = reset, keys + − 0. Works in 3D and 2D.
